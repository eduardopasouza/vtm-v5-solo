> Compiled from the 431-page V5 Core Rulebook PDF. Covers all major mechanical systems with exact numbers, costs, tables, and rules.

## 1. DICE POOL MECHANICS

### Building a Dice Pool
- Dice pool = **Attribute + Skill** (or Attribute + Discipline, or Attribute + Attribute)
- Roll ten-sided dice (d10s). Pools generally range from 1 to 10+ dice.
- Minimum pool size is always **1 die** (penalties can never reduce a pool below 1).
- A character lacking a Skill rolls only the pool's Attribute, with no additional penalty.
- **Specialties** add **+1 die** to the pool when the action falls within the specialty.

### Successes and Difficulties
- Every die result of **6 or higher = 1 success** (including 10, shown as 0).
- If successes >= Difficulty, the roll is a **win**.
- **Margin** = successes exceeding Difficulty (used for damage calculation and degree of effect).

### Difficulty Table
| Difficulty | Description |
|---|---|
| 1 | Routine |
| 2 | Straightforward |
| 3 | Moderate |
| 4 | Challenging |
| 5 | Hard |
| 6 | Very Hard |
| 7+ | Nearly Impossible |

### Critical Successes
- A pair of 10s on **regular dice** (two 10s) = **critical success** = **4 total successes** (the pair counts as 2 successes + 2 bonus).
- Three 10s = 5 successes; four 10s = 8 successes.
- A **critical win** = a winning roll containing at least one critical success.

### Messy Critical
- A critical win where **one or more 10s appear on a Hunger die** = **messy critical**.
- The character succeeds but in a bestial, uncontrolled way.
- Consequences (choose one or more): gain Stains, breach the Masquerade, lose a dot in an Advantage, or (if none fit) the test simply fails.
- Cannot occur on Willpower or Humanity rolls (no Hunger dice in those).

### Bestial Failure
- A **failed roll** (not enough successes) where **one or more Hunger dice show a 1** = **bestial failure**.
- Primary consequence: the character must act out a **Compulsion**.
- Alternative consequences: lose a dot in an Advantage, suffer Aggravated Health damage, or Hunger increases by 1 (if this would exceed 5, roll hunger frenzy at Difficulty 4).
- If the roll succeeds, no bestial failure occurs regardless of 1s on Hunger dice.

### Total Failure
- A roll with **zero successes** = total failure. Storyteller defines consequences.

### Win at a Cost
- If a roll has some successes but fails, Storyteller may offer a "win at a cost" -- you achieve the goal but suffer a complication.

### Willpower Rerolls
- Spend **1 Willpower** to re-roll **up to 3 regular dice** (not Hunger dice).
- Marks Superficial Willpower damage. Cannot re-roll Hunger dice, tracker rolls (Willpower, Humanity).

### Automatic Wins
- If a character's dice pool is **twice the Difficulty**, the Storyteller may rule an automatic win (margin = 0).

### Teamwork
- Roll the largest pool among participants, adding **+1 die per assistant** who has at least 1 dot in the relevant Skill.

### Trackers
- **Health = Stamina + 3**
- **Willpower = Composure + Resolve**

## 2. HUNGER MECHANICS

### Hunger Levels (0-5)
- **Hunger 0**: Sated and satisfied. Only achievable by **draining and killing a human**.
- **Hunger 1-4**: Rising levels of vampiric need.
- **Hunger 5**: Ravenous. Cannot intentionally Rouse the Blood. Forced Rouse Checks trigger immediate hunger frenzy test at Difficulty 4.

### Hunger Dice
- For each level of Hunger, the vampire replaces regular dice in their pool with Hunger dice (different color, suggested red) on a 1-for-1 basis.
- If the dice pool is **smaller than Hunger**, roll a number of Hunger dice equal to the pool.
- Hunger dice function as regular dice (6+ = success) BUT:
  - **Cannot be re-rolled** with Willpower.
  - **1 on a Hunger die** + failed roll = bestial failure.
  - **10 on a Hunger die** + critical win = messy critical.
- **Exception**: No Hunger dice in Checks, Willpower rolls, or Humanity rolls.

### Rouse Checks
- Roll **1 die**. Result of **6+ = success** (Hunger unchanged). **1-5 = failure** (Hunger increases by 1).
- Rouse Checks are required for: waking each sunset, Blood Surge, Blush of Life, mending damage, activating Discipline powers, and other Blood abilities.
- Higher Blood Potency allows rolling **2 dice and picking the highest** for Rouse Checks on certain Discipline levels.
- At **Hunger 5**: cannot intentionally Rouse the Blood; forced Rouse Checks trigger hunger frenzy test (Difficulty 4).
- **Effect still activates** even on a failed Rouse Check (only Hunger increases).

### Feeding / Slaking Hunger Table

| Source | Hunger Slaked | Time | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Small animals (3-4 cats, 12+ rats) | 1 | One scene | No effect above BP 2 |
| Medium animal (dog, raccoon) | 1 | One turn | No effect above BP 2 |
| Large animal (horse) | 2 | One scene | No effect above BP 2 |
| Blood bag | 1 | One turn | No effect above BP 2; no Resonance |
| Sip from human | 1 | Three turns | Includes licking wound closed |
| Max non-harmful human drink | 2 | One scene | -- |
| Harmful drink (risks death) | 1-4 | 1 turn/Hunger | Agg damage = Hunger slaked; human rolls Str+Sta vs. Difficulty = Hunger slaked |
| Human drained and killed | 5 | 5 turns | **Only way to reach Hunger 0** |

### Feeding from Other Vampires
- Slake 1 Hunger per 1 Hunger inflicted on the donor.
- If donor's BP is 2+ higher: slake 2 per 1 inflicted.
- If donor's BP is 2+ lower: slake 1 per 2 inflicted.
- Risks Blood Bond.

### Bite Attacks
- Declare intent to bite before rolling; remove **-2 dice** from the Brawl pool.
- Fangs deal **2 points of Aggravated Health damage** (to mortals and vampires alike).
- On subsequent turns after a successful bite: no penalty to Brawl pool; feeding does 1 Aggravated damage to mortals per turn and slakes 1 Hunger.

## 3. HUMANITY RULES

### The Humanity Track
- 10 spaces, from 0 to 10. Default starting value: **Humanity 7**.

### Chronicle Tenets
- Set by the troupe; apply to ALL player characters. These define what actions cause Stains.
- Sample sets: Humanist ("Thou shalt not kill," "Thou shalt not rape or torture," "Do not harm the innocent"), Creed of Justice, Gothic/Romantic, Street Code.

### Convictions
- 1-3 personal core values per character (e.g., "Never kill the innocent," "My family must be kept out of this").
- If a Tenet violation serves a Conviction, reduce Stains by 1 or more.

### Touchstones
- Equal number to Convictions. Must be **living human beings**.
- Each Touchstone is connected to one Conviction.
- If a Touchstone is destroyed and not transferred, the linked Conviction is lost.
- **Touchstone damage Stains**: damaged (+1), damaged by your actions (+2), destroyed (+2), destroyed by your actions (+3).

### Stains
- Tracked right-to-left on the Humanity track's empty spaces.
- Gained from violating chronicle Tenets (1-3+ Stains depending on severity), messy criticals, and Touchstone damage.
- Additional Stains: Blood Bonding a mortal (+1), Embracing a mortal (+2).

### Remorse
- At end of session, if any Stains are marked, roll **Remorse test**.
- Dice = unmarked, unfilled dots on the Humanity tracker (minimum 1 die).
- **1+ successes**: character retains current Humanity; remove all Stains.
- **No successes**: lose 1 Humanity; remove all Stains.

### Degeneration
- If Stains exceed empty boxes on the track: character is **Impaired** (-2 dice to all pools), takes 1 Aggravated Willpower damage per excess Stain.
- While Impaired by degeneration: cannot intentionally violate Tenets; forced violations trigger terror frenzy (Difficulty 4).
- Can snap out of it by voluntarily losing 1 Humanity.

### Humanity Effects Table

| Humanity | Dice Added to Resist Frenzy | Torpor Length |
|---|---|---|
| 9 | 3 | Three days |
| 8 | 2 | One week |
| 7 | 2 | Two weeks |
| 6 | 2 | One month |
| 5 | 1 | One year |
| 4 | 1 | One decade |
| 3 | 1 | Five decades |
| 2 | 0 | One century |
| 1 | 0 | Five centuries |

- **Humanity 0**: character goes into a spectacular frenzy (all Attributes to 5), becomes a wight (SPC).
- **Humanity 9+**: vampire appears ill/"heroin chic" rather than dead; does not need Blush of Life for basic purposes.

## 4. BLOOD POTENCY TABLE

| BP | Blood Surge | Damage Mended/Rouse | Discipline Power Bonus | Discipline Rouse Re-roll | Bane Severity | Feeding Penalty |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | None | 1 Superficial | None | None | 0 | No effect |
| 1 | +1 die | 1 Superficial | None | Level 1 | 1 | No effect |
| 2 | +1 die | 2 Superficial | +1 die | Level 1 | 1 | Animal/bagged slakes half |
| 3 | +2 dice | 2 Superficial | +1 die | Level 2 and below | 2 | Animal/bagged slakes nothing |
| 4 | +2 dice | 3 Superficial | +2 dice | Level 2 and below | 2 | Slake 1 less per human |
| 5 | +3 dice | 3 Superficial | +2 dice | Level 3 and below | 3 | Slake 1 less per human; must kill to go below Hunger 2 |
| 6 | +3 dice | 3 Superficial | +3 dice | Level 3 and below | 3 | Slake 2 less per human; must kill to go below Hunger 2 |
| 7 | +4 dice | 3 Superficial | +3 dice | Level 4 and below | 4 | -- |
| 8 | +4 dice | 4 Superficial | +4 dice | Level 4 and below | 4 | Slake 2 less; must kill to go below Hunger 3 |
| 9 | +5 dice | 4 Superficial | +4 dice | Level 5 and below | 5 | -- |
| 10 | +5 dice | 5 Superficial | +5 dice | Level 5 and below | 5 | Slake 3 less; must kill to go below Hunger 3 |

### Blood Potency and Generation
- BP increases by 1 per ~100 years active; decreases by 1 per 50 years in torpor.
- Cannot fall below generation minimum or exceed maximum.
- Thin-bloods (14th-16th gen) = BP 0; cannot increase unless they diablerize to 13th gen.

### Blood Surge
- Add dice (per BP table) to any one Attribute-based dice pool.
- Requires a Rouse Check. Applies to a single roll only. Cannot be used on Willpower/Humanity rolls or One-Roll Combat.

### Mending Damage
- **Superficial**: 1 Rouse Check removes points per BP table. One Rouse Check per turn.
- **Aggravated**: Wait until next nightfall, make **3 Rouse Checks** (plus the regular waking Rouse Check). Removes 1 Aggravated damage per night. If Rouse Checks push Hunger above 5, vampire falls into torpor.

### Blush of Life
- Costs 1 Rouse Check. Lasts one night.
- Allows: heartbeat, body temp, breath, touch screens, consuming food (up to 1 hour), sexual intercourse.

## 5. PREDATOR TYPES (All with Exact Mechanical Effects)

### Alleycat
- Specialty: Intimidation (Stickups) OR Brawl (Grappling)
- +1 dot Celerity or Potence
- -1 Humanity
- +3 dots criminal Contacts

### Bagger
- Specialty: Larceny (Lockpicking) OR Streetwise (Black Market)
- +1 dot Blood Sorcery (Tremere only) or Obfuscate
- Gain Iron Gullet Merit (3 dots)
- Gain Enemy Flaw (2 dots)
- Ventrue may NOT pick this type

### Blood Leech
- Specialty: Brawl (Kindred) OR Stealth (against Kindred)
- +1 dot Celerity or Protean
- -1 Humanity
- +1 Blood Potency
- Gain Dark Secret Flaw (2 dots): Diablerist, OR Shunned Flaw (2 dots)
- Gain Feeding Flaw (2 dots): Prey Exclusion (mortals)

### Cleaver
- Specialty: Persuasion (Gaslighting) OR Subterfuge (Coverups)
- +1 dot Dominate or Animalism
- Gain Dark Secret Flaw (1 dot): Cleaver
- Gain Herd Advantage (2 dots)

### Consensualist
- Specialty: Medicine (Phlebotomy) OR Persuasion (Victims)
- +1 dot Auspex or Fortitude
- +1 Humanity
- Gain Dark Secret Flaw (1 dot): Masquerade Breacher
- Gain Feeding Flaw (1 dot): Prey Exclusion (non-consenting)

### Farmer
- Specialty: Animal Ken (Specific Animal) OR Survival (Hunting)
- +1 dot Animalism or Protean
- +1 Humanity
- Gain Feeding Flaw (2 dots): Vegan
- Ventrue may NOT pick. Cannot pick if BP >= 3.

### Osiris
- Specialty: Occult (specific tradition) OR Performance (specific field)
- +1 dot Blood Sorcery (Tremere only) or Presence
- Spend 3 dots between Fame and Herd
- Spend 2 dots between Enemies and Mythic Flaws

### Sandman
- Specialty: Medicine (Anesthetics) OR Stealth (Break-in)
- +1 dot Auspex or Obfuscate
- +1 dot Resources

### Scene Queen
- Specialty: Etiquette (specific scene), Leadership (specific scene), OR Streetwise (specific scene)
- +1 dot Dominate or Potence
- Gain Fame Advantage (1 dot)
- Gain Contact Advantage (1 dot)
- Gain Influence Flaw (1 dot): Disliked (outside subculture) OR Feeding Flaw (1 dot): Prey Exclusion (different subculture)

### Siren
- Specialty: Persuasion (Seduction) OR Subterfuge (Seduction)
- +1 dot Fortitude or Presence
- Gain Looks Merit (2 dots): Beautiful
- Gain Enemy Flaw (1 dot): spurned lover or jealous partner

## 6. CHARACTER CREATION STEPS AND COSTS

### Step-by-Step Summary

1. **Core Concept**: Name, background, when/where Embraced.
2. **Clan and Sire**: Pick clan. Note sire on Relationship Map.
3. **Attributes**: One at 4; three at 3; four at 2; one at 1. (Health = Stamina + 3; Willpower = Composure + Resolve)
4. **Skills** (pick one distribution):
   - **Jack of All Trades**: 1 at 3; 8 at 2; 10 at 1
   - **Balanced**: 3 at 3; 5 at 2; 7 at 1
   - **Specialist**: 1 at 4; 3 at 3; 3 at 2; 3 at 1
   - Free specialties for Academics, Craft, Performance, Science + 1 additional free specialty.
5. **Disciplines**: Choose 2 of your clan Disciplines. Put 2 dots in one, 1 dot in the other. (Caitiff: any 2 Disciplines.)
6. **Predator Type**: Apply specialty, Discipline dot, Advantages/Flaws.
7. **Advantages**: Spend **7 points** on Advantages; take **2 points of Flaws** (in addition to Predator Type flaws).
8. **Convictions & Touchstones**: Select 1-3 Convictions; create equal Touchstones. Set Humanity to **7**.
9. **Sea of Time** (Generation/Age):
   - **Childer** (Embraced within 15 years): 14th-16th gen = BP 0; 12th-13th gen = BP 1
   - **Neonates** (Embraced 1940-decade ago): 12th-13th gen = BP 1; spend **15 XP**
   - **Ancillae** (Embraced 1780-1940): 10th-11th gen = BP 2; +2 Advantages, +2 Flaws, -1 Humanity; spend **35 XP**

## 8. COMBAT RULES

### Basic Conflict
- Each participant declares intent at start of turn.
- Attacker and defender roll simultaneously in a contest.
- Winner's margin (successes minus opponent's successes) = **damage** to a tracker (Health or Willpower).
- **Weapons** add their damage rating to margin.
- A tie in two-sided combat = both inflict 1 damage on each other.

### Who Goes First (Basic)
- Close combat (already engaged) > ranged combat > newly initiated close combat > everything else.
- Ties: compare Dexterity + Wits, then dots of Skill used.

### Who Goes First (Advanced/Optional)
- **Initiative = Composure + Awareness** (static, no roll).
- Highest goes first; descending order. Vampires before mortals on ties.

### Close Combat
- **Unarmed**: Strength + Brawl
- **One-handed weapons**: Dexterity + Melee
- **Two-handed weapons**: Strength + Melee
- **Defense/Dodge**: Dexterity + Athletics (no damage inflicted on win)

### Grappling
- Strength + Brawl to grapple (win = restrain, no damage).
- Next round, grappler contests Str + Brawl again: if win, choose damage, bite (2 Aggravated), or hold.
- Bite attacks on grappled foe: no -2 penalty.

### Ranged Combat
- Standard: **Composure + Firearms**; sniper = Resolve + Firearms; showdown = Dexterity + Firearms.
- Defense: **Dexterity + Athletics** (modified by cover).
- Cover modifiers: No cover (-2), concealment only (-1), hard cover (+/-0), entrenchment (+1), murder hole (+2).
- Firearms in close combat: Strength + Firearms, -2 penalty for targeting outside scuffle, -2 for weapons larger than pistol.
- Beyond effective range: -2 dice penalty.

### Multiple Opponents
- Lose 1 die from defense pool for each successive attacker.
- Must split pool to attack multiple foes.

### Damage Types
- **Superficial**: bruising, sprains, non-lethal. Halve (round up) before applying. Fists and standard weapons vs. vampires.
- **Aggravated**: broken bones, lethal injury. Applied in full. Fire, sunlight, supernatural claws/teeth vs. vampires; sharp/piercing weapons vs. mortals.

### Impairment
- When a tracker is completely filled (any combination of damage): character is **Impaired** (-2 dice to relevant pools).
- Further damage converts existing Superficial to Aggravated.
- Tracker completely filled with Aggravated: comatose/dead (mortals) or **torpor** (vampires).

### All-Out Attack (Optional)
- +1 damage bonus, but cannot defend against any attacks that turn.

### All-Out Defense (Optional)
- +1 bonus die to all defense rolls, but cannot attack.

### Called Shots
- Declare target before rolling; subtract successes (usually -2) after testing.

### One-Roll Conflicts
- Set Difficulty based on opposition strength (2/4/6). Each PC rolls once (no Willpower rerolls or Blood Surge).
- Damage = (2x Difficulty) - successes. Not halved even if Superficial.

### Three, Two, Done
- After 3 rounds, assess if conflict should conclude. Options: concession, one final roll, or fast-forward.

## 9. DISCIPLINE COSTS AND POWER LEVELS

### General Rules
- Discipline powers are rated **Level 1 through Level 5** (core book).
- Activating most powers costs **at least 1 Rouse Check**.
- A vampire can activate **one Discipline power per turn**.
- Higher Blood Potency grants re-rolls on Rouse Checks for lower-level powers (see BP table).
- **Discipline Power Bonus** (from BP) adds dice when using or resisting Discipline powers.

### Core Book Disciplines
- **Animalism** (Gangrel, Ministry, Nosferatu, Ravnos)
- **Auspex** (Hecata, Malkavian, Toreador, Tremere)
- **Blood Sorcery** (Banu Haqim, Tremere)
- **Celerity** (Banu Haqim, Brujah, Toreador)
- **Dominate** (Lasombra, Malkavian, Tremere, Ventrue)
- **Fortitude** (Gangrel, Hecata, Ventrue)
- **Obfuscate** (Banu Haqim, Malkavian, Ministry, Nosferatu)
- **Potence** (Brujah, Lasombra, Nosferatu)
- **Presence** (Brujah, Ministry, Toreador, Ventrue)
- **Protean** (Gangrel, Ministry)
- **Blood Sorcery Rituals** (separate from powers; cost = Ritual Level x 3 XP)
- **Thin-Blood Alchemy** (thin-bloods only; cost = Formula Level x 3 XP)

### XP Costs
- **In-clan Discipline**: New level x 5 (e.g., level 1 = 5 XP, level 2 = 10 XP, level 3 = 15 XP)
- **Out-of-clan Discipline**: New level x 7
- **Caitiff Discipline**: New level x 6

## 10. COMPULSIONS (from Bestial Failure)

### Random Compulsions Table (roll d10)
| Roll | Compulsion |
|---|---|
| 1-3 | **Hunger** |
| 4-5 | **Dominance** |
| 6-7 | **Harm** |
| 8-9 | **Paranoia** |
| 0 (10) | **Clan Compulsion** (re-roll for Caitiff or thin-blood) |

### Compulsion Effects
- **Hunger**: -2 dice to all pools not immediately conducive to feeding. Ends when 1+ Hunger slaked.
- **Dominance**: -2 dice to pools not establishing dominance. Cannot use teamwork. Ends when vampire has "won" and gloated.
- **Harm**: -2 dice to pools not resulting in harm. Ends when someone/something is incapacitated, destroyed, or driven away.
- **Paranoia**: (follows similar pattern -- -2 dice penalty to actions not addressing perceived threat).

### Key Rule
- Players **cannot spend Willpower** to ignore Compulsions (unlike frenzy).
- Playing into a Compulsion well may restore 1 Willpower.

## 11. GIFTS OF THE BLOOD (Summary)

- **Blush of Life**: 1 Rouse Check. Appear human for 1 night.
- **Blood Surge**: 1 Rouse Check. Add dice (per BP) to one Attribute roll.
- **Mending Superficial damage**: 1 Rouse Check per attempt (1/turn). Heals per BP table.
- **Mending Aggravated damage**: 3 Rouse Checks + waking Rouse Check. 1 Aggravated per night.
- **Waking at sunset**: 1 Rouse Check.
